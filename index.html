Fixing Ember Mug on Google Pixel

Google Pixel's Android Bluetooth stack enforces strict GATT security and drops idle unbonded BLE connections. Follow these steps once for a rock-solid connection:

  1. Force Close or Uninstall the Official Ember App: The official Ember app runs a background scanner that fights for exclusive GATT control and resets your connection.
  2. Put Your Ember Mug in Pairing Mode: Press and hold the power button on the bottom of your mug for 6–7 seconds until the LED blinks blue.
  3. Bond in Pixel Bluetooth Settings (Optional but Recommended): If Android shows a "Pair with Ember Ceramic Mug?" notification when connecting, tap Pair. Bonding allows writing target temperatures and enables push notifications.
  4. Tap "Connect Ember Mug" in this App: Select your mug from Chrome's chooser. Our built-in keep-alive engine polls every 6 seconds and automatically reconnects if the Pixel radio hiccups.
